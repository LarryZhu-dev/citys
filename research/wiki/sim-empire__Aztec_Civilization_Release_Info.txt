# Aztec Civilization Release Info

You may know that there was a release planned for August 31st to add a 5th civilization, the Aztecs.  It was announced on the Chinese-language website for the game, so it doesn't help us non-Chinese speaking people very much.  The app update last week was in preparation, and you may have seen some of the Aztec materials show up at the bottom of the list of goods/materials that can be purchased in the market (though there is none available currently, since nobody can produce any of it yet).

In addition to the new civilization, the developers are apparently going to be increasing the market price cap, optimizing some battle mechanics, and are planning on eventually introducing cross-server wars, though details of these haven't been released yet.  

Bare in mind, some of the information I receive on this is second- or third-hand, so it may not be entirely accurate, and until it is released the developers can always change their mind.
