# Starting Game Experience

[[File:Civilization1.png|frameless|475x475px]]

As you enter the game for the first time (or after you resetting your Civilization) you will see the Civilization Select window. 

* Egypt
* Greece
* Persia
* China

Chose whichever civilization you want to build up, if you decide later that you do not like the civilization you chose, you can reset your game with a free reset provided by the system, however do decide early on, resetting when you are higher level is frustrating. If you ever feel you want to reset and your account is higher level, it maybe better to just start another civilization in either another server or another account .
[[File:Chapterintro.png|thumb]]


Chapters: In this game your level is called chapter, meaning you have completed a list of tasks in the long list of tasks that will be given to you as your progress in the game. 
[[File:Consultant.png|thumb]]
The chapters intro has a little bit of information on why you are getting the tasks you are getting next, and an overall description of what you will accomplish. 


Consultant: This useful and pesky character will give you warning, advise and instructions on what to do in game. She is handy, but can get annoying later on. 
<br />
[[File:Tasks.png|thumb]]
Tasks: Given by different system characters, these NEED to be completed in order to go forward in game. Pay close attention to the instructions, they will give you a hint of where to locate what you need to do, what technology needs to be learned, and also pay attention to the amount of things they request, ie. It may ask you to build a farm, not 2 farms, but 1 farm. This is important as when you are just starting your resources and your workers (population) is very limited, so DO NOT OVERBUILD!
