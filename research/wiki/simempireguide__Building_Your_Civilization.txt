# Building Your Civilization

These are the first steps to get your civilization going. 

* Building
**To build anything you need to select the Hammer icon then from the selection menu chose the category you want to build from, select the building and DRAG the building into the are where you want to place it. Your building will start only after you have checked the check mark icon, you can cancel the attempt by clicking on the x mark icon.
**Radius: Some buildings have a radius of coverage, make sure that that radius covers the building you want to get benefit from it (they will glow green if they are within the radius), this is very important to pay attention to, you will want to maximize the usage of those buildings, try to build the least amount that cover a maximum area, ie. If you build a water well, make sure that all your residences are glowing green. Another example is if you build a Fire prevention building, make sure all the buildings that need protection are glowing green (decorations or other safety municipal buildings do not need protection from safety buildings).

'''IMPORTANT:''' Although there is an option to destroy a building, please do not use it unless you absolutely have to, such as when a building has been destroyed by not being covered by a protection building, or when you are in dire need for gold and want a refund (just wait for your tax levy if this is the case!). Instead of destroying, just move the building by using the option to "Pick Up" building, it will save you tons of money and resources. If the building you want to move is a production building, you need to stop the production before the icon to move the building is visible. Tip: Decorations can be moved also, roads can't those have to be destroyed.
