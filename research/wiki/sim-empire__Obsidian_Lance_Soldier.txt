# Obsidian Lance Soldier

[[File:Screenshot 20201011-182900 01 01.jpg|center|frameless|601x601px]]


EvDigg's Thoughts:

Compared to the entry-level melee troops of other civilizations it is fairly strong and counterattack is rare in low-level melee troops.  It is hurt however by it's high effective cost, as it requires 2 weapons and weapons are usually quite expensive in the Aztec civilization in most market conditions (copper has to be much more expensive than wood and wood has to be much more expensive than obsidian for the cost of weapons to be similar to those of other civilizations).  

Its attack, defense and health are stronger than entry-level, but weaker than most mid-level troops.  If their skills are fully upgraded they can attack multiple targets, but even with this and their counterattack I don't feel they'd find much effective use in most armies.
