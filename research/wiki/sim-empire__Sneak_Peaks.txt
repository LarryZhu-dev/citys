# Sneak Peaks

This page will contain all rumors and announcements that have come out regarding unreleased functionality that is believed will be added to the game in the near future.  Currently there has been information trickling in about a 5th civilization (Aztec) that was supposed to be added yesterday (August 31, 2020) but has been delayed.  

This information is released in the game's chat forums and on the developer's websites, but it is only released in Chinese, so you'll have to excuse the somewhat less-than-ideal translation work of Google Translate.
