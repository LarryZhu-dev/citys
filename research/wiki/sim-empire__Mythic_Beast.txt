EvDigg's Thoughts:

While the Mythical Beast is stronger and faster than most troops, I wouldn't include it among my list of the strongest troops that can dominate an enemy in a war.  It is above average, but nothing spectacular.  It doesn't require weapons though, so it's true cost is low compared to other troops of similar power, health and speed.  Its speed is fast as long as it isn't going up against speed 9 troops, and it's 55 health points make it harder to kill than any of the horse troops - though it also does less damage than many of them.  

This troop isn't seen much in battles even though it is a fairly good all-around troop, which I suspect is due to it not being as fast as the Chinese horse troops and it is overshadowed by the much more powerful Goddess Nuwa.
